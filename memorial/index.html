Education & Training

Naveed Ahmad Anjum held an Associate Engineering diploma in Instrumentation & Control Automation. He continually pursued excellence through international training, completing both basic and advanced courses in Biomedical Engineering (PLC-based electronic equipment) in Germany (1989 and 1993).

He also underwent practical training in CNG equipment in New Zealand (2003) and Italy (2003, 2004, and 2005). His expertise was further enhanced through specialized training in Energy Conversion & Control, Electrical and Electronic Automation Systems, and LPG & CNG Conversions and Operating Systems.

Career

Naveed Ahmad Anjum was a highly respected engineering and business professional whose career spanned more than three decades across the fields of electrical engineering, instrumentation, automation, and alternative energy systems. He was known for his deep technical knowledge, leadership abilities, and commitment to innovation in engineering and industrial management.

He served as the Chief Operating Officer at Naveed Engineering Services (Pvt) Ltd. from 2003 and at Hotel Corporate Inn from 2006. Over the years, he held several key leadership and technical roles, including:

  • Technical Director — Motorway CNG Operators Company (Pvt) Ltd., Lahore
  • Chief Operating Officer (COO) — Nasco Engineering Services and Nasco Enterprises, Lahore
  • Managing Officer — Elettrogas FZC, Sharjah, UAE
  • Project Manager (E&I) — Shazeb Pharmaceutical Industries, Haripur (working alongside a Finnish team)
  • Maintenance Manager (E&I) — ATS Synthetic, Lahore (collaborating with an Italian team)
  • Senior Sales and Service Engineer — Medi Pak Ltd., Lahore (9 years)
  • Engineer — ADNOC, Abu Dhabi
  • Engineer — ARAMCO, Saudi Arabia
  • Engineer — Pakistan Steel, Karachi

Naveed Ahmad Anjum was widely recognized for his comprehensive knowledge of LPG and CNG systems, as well as his keen insight into process automation and engineering solutions. He traveled extensively, both within Pakistan and abroad, to remain at the forefront of technological advancements in his field.
